Challenge 1 · Doubling Paid Show-Up Rate
Paid media show-up must jump from 27.8% to ~50%

Last year stripped survey questions to chase raw registration counts, pulling in low-intent leads and dragging down attendance.

Breakthrough 1 · Paid Voucher Filter + Deep Survey Algorithm Feedback

Reinstate real-name ID checks plus a 5–6 question structured trade survey fed back to Tencent V3 lookalike models (proven at 47%–55% show-up in comparable expos), paired with paid product-voucher passes and zero-cost peer referrals.

Challenge 2 · 2-Month Cooling Gap & Audience Mismatch
One-size-fits-all reminders alienate executives and bore buyers

Early registrants registered in Feb–Mar forget by Apr 21; blasting identical promotional copy drives C-suite opt-outs while failing to answer voucher buyer questions.

Breakthrough 2 · Automated 5-Tier Community Nurturing

Capture 70%–80% of registrants into a dedicated WeCom pool within 5 seconds of registration, auto-route by survey role into 5 distinct communities with tailored frequency and hooks, adding +10–15 pts to attendance.

Challenge 3 · Account Bans & Cross-Contamination Risk
High-volume community onboarding & 1-on-1 blasts threaten core sales accounts

WeChat enforces tight friend-add and messaging ceilings; mixing bulk outreach with core sales accounts risks cascading bans across company assets.

Breakthrough 3 · Physical 4-Zone Isolation + Official Channel Offload

Keep core sales accounts untouched (Red Zone); isolate communities on a separate WeCom entity with 10-seat routing (Green Zone); run bulk 1-on-1 blasts on dedicated devices/SIMs with 30% warm backups (Amber Zone); move 70% of routine notices to SMS + Service Account AI (Blue Zone).

Challenge 4 · Tasting Promo vs. On-Site Paid Tasting Revenue
Free tasting giveaways can cannibalize paid tasting sales & clog peak queues

Giving unrestricted "first-come, first-served" free tasting slots risks converting paying buyers into freebie seekers, crowding 11:00–15:00 peak hours, and sparking internal quota disputes.

Breakthrough 4 · Conflict-Free Tasting Design (Off-Peak / Sample-to-Sale / Internal Buyout)

Never promote free tastings publicly. Limit as an invite-only perk inside C-Suite/Buyer groups using one of 3 revenue-safe models: off-peak/surplus time slots, single-sample or discount vouchers that drive paid upsells, or fixed internal cost-price buyouts with 15:00 auto-release.

Challenge 5 · Data Sovereignty & Voucher Fulfillment Risk
Agency Mini-Programs vs. unified master data, plus voucher oversell & invoice rework

Fragmented registration systems break deduplication; uncapped hot brand vouchers cause fulfillment crises; pre-show invoicing forces mass credit notes on no-show refunds.

Breakthrough 5 · Contracted Real-Time Sync + 80% Pool Lock + Post-Show Invoicing

Approved rules lock real-time data sync into one master sheet, real-time inventory deduction with 80% BD top-up alerts & 100% fallback bundles, and post-show invoicing strictly after verified gate scan.

Optional Attendance Hook (Conflict-Free Redesign): Protecting Paid Tasting Sales While Lifting C-Suite & Buyer Attendance

Why redesign? Offering unrestricted free tasting slots risks cannibalizing paying customers, congesting peak selling hours (11:00–15:00), and creating quota friction if sales surge. If management approves linking the tasting zone, adopt one of the three revenue-safe models below—strictly invite-only inside Tier 1 & 2 communities, never advertised publicly.

Option A (Recommended): Off-Peak & Surplus Allocation Keep peak hours (11:00–15:00) 100% reserved for full-price sales. Open invite-only slots during 09:30–10:30 (builds early crowd energy) and 15:30–16:30 (converts daily perishable surplus with zero waste).
Option B (Revenue-Accretive): Taster Sample or Credit Voucher Instead of gifting full paid tasting packages, issue a single-item preview sample or RMB 15–20 off credit. VIPs still receive a clear perk while driving incremental paid conversions directly to the tasting booth.
Option C (Zero Friction): Internal Cost-Price Buyout Visitor acquisition budget buys a fixed daily block (e.g., 50–100 slots) at internal cost. Tasting team secures guaranteed revenue; any unredeemed slots auto-release at 15:00 for regular paid resale.
Rollout Guardrail: Pending leadership approval of the tasting business plan; keep Day 1 (Apr 21) redemption rules strict and tune Day 2–3 capacity based on live Day-1 sales velocity.

4. Four-Zone Account Isolation & Anti-Ban Architecture (Zero Spillover Risk)

Hard Rule: Core business accounts never run bulk blasts; community accounts stay strictly compliant on an isolated WeCom entity; bulk 1-on-1 outreach uses physically separated burner pools; routine notices offload to zero-ban official channels.

Red Zone · Core Business Assets

Sales Leads / BD / Executives / Key Client Accounts
  • Permitted: Authentic 1-on-1 relationship management, VIP follow-up, and normal Moments updates.
  • Hard Ban: Zero third-party automation plugins; zero public-ad lead routing; zero mass 1-on-1 blasting.
  • Result: Completely insulated from user complaints and platform penalties.

Green Zone · Community Service Pool

Isolated Sub-Entity WeCom + 10-Seat Official Routing QR
  • Mission: Absorb post-registration friend adds, auto-route into the 5 communities, run scheduled drops & AI FAQ.
  • Compliance: Official WeCom live codes, official welcome messages, and native group broadcasts only—zero grey-hat automation.
  • Isolation: Hosted under a separate WeCom entity so complaints never impact main corporate WeCom.

Amber Zone · 1-on-1 Outreach Pool

Dedicated Phones + Independent SIMs + 30% Warm Backups
  • Mission: Execute high-frequency 1-on-1 ticket invites, private blasts, and reactivation nudges.
  • Physical Separation: Dedicated hardware, cellular data (off corporate Wi-Fi), and separate IDs—zero cross-account contagion if a single seat is restricted.
  • Safety SOP: Warm up accounts 30 days ahead; pilot every wave on 500 users; separate text & poster sends; strictly deduplicate registrants before every send.

Blue Zone · Zero-Ban Official Channels

SMS Gateway + WeChat Service Account Templates + AI Support
  • Mission: Handle verification results, 4-wave pre-show attendance reminders, post-show voucher code dispatch, auto-refund alerts, and self-service queries.
  • Impact: Offloads ~70% of mechanical messaging away from personal/WeCom accounts while leveraging SMS's proven 50.3% show-up efficiency.
Automated Circuit Breaker: Track daily friend-accept rate, complaint count, and delivery failures across Green & Amber pools. If any account shows throttling, repeated captchas, or ≥ 2 complaints/day, auto-freeze for 48 hours and fail over live QR traffic to the 30% warm backup pool within 60 seconds.
